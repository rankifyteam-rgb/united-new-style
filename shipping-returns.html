Shipping & Returns

Everything you need to know about receiving and returning your order.

Shipping Policy

United New Style offers complimentary worldwide shipping on all orders. Orders are typically processed within 1–2 business days and delivered within 5–10 business days depending on your destination.

Once your order ships, you will receive a confirmation email with tracking information.

Returns Policy

We want you to love your United New Style pieces. If you are not fully satisfied, you may return unworn items with original tags attached within 30 days of delivery for a full refund or exchange.

  • Items must be unworn, unwashed and in their original packaging.
  • Sale items are final sale unless otherwise stated.
  • Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5–7 business days of receiving the return.

How to Start a Return

Email info@unitednewstyle.com with your order number and we will guide you through the process.
